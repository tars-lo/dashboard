# Banana Oat Pancakes

Simple, nutritious pancakes perfect for baby-led weaning. No added sugar — sweetness comes from ripe banana.

## Ingredients

- 1 ripe banana
- 1 egg
- ½ cup rolled oats (blitzed to flour)
- 2 tbsp whole milk or formula
- Pinch of cinnamon (optional)
- Butter or coconut oil for cooking

## Instructions

1. Mash banana in a bowl until smooth
2. Beat in the egg
3. Add oat flour, milk, and cinnamon. Mix until combined
4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Add a small amount of butter
5. Drop spoonfuls of batter (~2 tbsp each). Cook 2 min per side until golden
6. Cool slightly before serving — these hold heat!

## Serving

- Cut into strips for self-feeding
- Serve with yogurt or mashed berries on the side

## Storage

- Fridge: 3 days in airtight container
- Freezer: 1 month. Reheat in toaster or microwave

## Notes

- Use very ripe banana (brown spots = sweeter)
- Can sub oat flour with regular flour if no allergy concerns
- Double batch and freeze for quick breakfasts
